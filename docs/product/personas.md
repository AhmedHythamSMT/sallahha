# Personas — Sallahha FieldOps

## P1. Karim, the AC workshop owner (Cairo / Giza, 35–50)
- Runs 3–8 technicians, takes orders via WhatsApp + phone. Uses paper or memory for assignments.
- Goals: no lost orders in summer peak, know who is free, prove work to argue payment.
- Pains: repeat customer calls "where is he?"; technicians forget photos/parts; no history.
- Tech: Android mid-range, good Arabic literacy, low patience for complex apps.
- MVP promise: one queue, one-tap assign, overdue flags, completion proof.

## P2. Mona, the dispatcher (often owner's relative/secretary)
- Triages WhatsApp all day, calls technicians, reschedules.
- Goals: fast triage (<2 min/request), correct technician first time.
- Pains: vague addresses, duplicate requests, customers who don't answer.
- Needs: search by phone, priority + SLA due, reassign in 2 taps, templates for confirmations.

## P3. Hassan, the field technician (20–35, diploma or apprenticeship)
- 4–8 visits/day in summer; weak mobile data in basements/rooftops/new compounds; low-end phone.
- Goals: clear address + problem before leaving; minimal typing on site.
- Pains: app that dies offline; long forms; battery drain; unclear spare-part availability.
- Needs: offline job card, big status buttons, photo capture, parts checklist, "waiting for parts" state.

## P4. Salma, the customer (apartment / villa / small office)
- Books AC repair once or twice a year; prefers WhatsApp/voice.
- Goals: book in <3 min, know arrival, trust the price, easy rebooking.
- Pains: no-shows, surprise costs, no receipt/proof, having to re-explain.
- Needs: simple form, photo optional, time slot, tracking timeline, confirm + rate.

## Anti-personas (explicitly NOT designing for in MVP)
- Enterprise facility managers (need SSO/SLA contracts — post-MVP).
- Marketplace bargain-hunters (no bidding/marketplace).
- Accountants (no ledger; estimate only).
